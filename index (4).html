Compliance & Trust

Operating Within Canadian
Regulatory Frameworks

As an Ontario-incorporated company, DM International Inc. adheres to Canadian federal and provincial regulations governing IT services, data privacy, and business operations.

🇨🇦 Privacy Legislation Awareness

We are familiar with the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA), Canada's federal privacy law, and design our client engagements with data sensitivity and confidentiality in mind.

🏛️ Ontario Incorporated Business

DM International Inc. is incorporated and operates under Ontario provincial law, giving clients a locally accountable, Canadian business partner for their IT needs.

🛡️ Security-Conscious Practices

Our team draws on widely recognized frameworks such as NIST and CIS Controls when advising clients on security posture — helping you make informed decisions about risk management.

🏥 Regulated Industry Experience

We have worked with clients in regulated sectors including healthcare and financial services, and understand the operational sensitivities that come with those environments.

📋 Accessibility Awareness (AODA)

When developing applications for Ontario organizations, we help clients think through the requirements of the Accessibility for Ontarians with Disabilities Act (AODA) so they can make informed decisions about inclusive design.

🤝 Responsible Vendor Relationships

We work transparently with clients on technology selection, ensuring you understand the tools and platforms we recommend — and how they fit within your organization's own governance and compliance obligations.
